Meditation Station

Give employees an opportunity to pause, reset, and recharge during the workday. Led by a yoga or meditation instructor, this station features brief guided meditation and relaxation breathing sessions designed to reduce stress, improve focus, and promote a sense of calm. Sessions are offered throughout the event, making it easy for participants to step away, practice mindfulness, and return feeling refreshed.

📋 Module 01

Before the Event

Session timing and supplies vary by event. Review your event notes before you arrive.

Event notes checklist

  • Session schedule — how long each session runs and how often
  • Supplies — whether US Wellness is shipping supplies for your event
  • Location and arrival time — where your station is and when to arrive for setup

What to wear

Instructors wear athletic clothing. It must be full coverage and not tight.

✓ Wear
  • Full-coverage athletic clothing
  • Athletic shoes
  • US Wellness name badge, if provided
✗ Don't wear
  • Tight or form-fitting clothing
  • Crop tops, sports bras worn alone, or short shorts
  • Jeans
🛠️ Module 02

Setting Up the Space

Your setup depends on whether US Wellness ships supplies for your event. Either way, the goal is the same: a quiet, comfortable space where participants can settle in quickly.

Illustration — participants face the instructor in the quietest area available.

If US Wellness ships supplies

  1. Confirm the shipment arrived and everything listed in your event notes is there.
  2. Arrange the supplies so participants face you, with enough room to sit comfortably.
  3. Set up signage showing session times, if provided.

If no supplies are shipped

  1. Find the quietest area available — away from speakers, foot traffic, and food stations.
  2. Use the seating that's there — chairs work well for brief seated meditation and breathing.
  3. Make your session times easy to find, for example by letting the event organizer know when sessions start.
🧘 Module 03

Leading Sessions

Sessions are brief guided meditation and relaxation breathing practices, run on the schedule in your event notes. Keep them accessible to everyone — no experience needed.

Session flow

  1. Welcome participants and let them know they can join or leave at any time.
  2. Invite them to get comfortable — feet on the floor, hands resting, eyes closed or softly focused.
  3. Guide relaxation breathing to help everyone settle in.
  4. Lead a brief guided meditation using your professional judgment and the time available.
  5. Close gently — invite participants to notice how they feel, and share a simple technique they can use at work.

Between sessions

  • Greet people who walk by and let them know when the next session starts.
  • Answer questions about meditation and breathing.
  • Reset the space so it's ready for the next group.

Conversation starters

No script needed — pick whatever fits the moment.

Inviting people in

  • "Our next session starts in a few minutes — want to take a short break with us?"
  • "Have you ever tried guided meditation? No experience needed."
  • "Need a moment to reset? It's just a few minutes."

After a session

  • "How do you feel compared to when you sat down?"
  • "When during your workday could you use that breathing technique?"
  • "What usually helps you unwind after a stressful day?"
💬
Keep it general

Meditation and breathing are wellness practices, not treatment. If someone shares that they're struggling with stress, anxiety, or their mental health, listen kindly and encourage them to reach out to their healthcare provider or their employer's employee assistance program.

📦 Module 04

Breakdown & Cleanup

End-of-event teardown — leave the space as calm and clean as you found it.

  1. If supplies were shipped, pack them up and handle them as described in your event notes.
  2. Return any furniture you moved to where you found it.
  3. Leave the space clean.
